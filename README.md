## The joke

> Our AI judge has never survived a Monday, yet it just promoted the printer, the only coworker that jams under pressure and never calls in sick.

One line about office life, 143 characters — under the 200-character limit. The joke plays on the judge itself: an AI that has never survived a Monday hands a promotion to the office printer, the one coworker that jams under pressure and never calls in sick.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the joke to `$ENTRY_OUT` (a `.txt` file, or `entry.txt` inside a directory), `$ENTRY_OUT_DIR/entry.txt`, or `/output/entry.txt` by default, and prints it to stdout. Standard library only, no network.
